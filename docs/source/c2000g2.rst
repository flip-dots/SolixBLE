C2000(X) G2
===========

The C2000(X) Gen 2 has one expansion slot, for a BP2000 or BP3000. Its
``c490`` device summary (:attr:`~SolixBLE.C2000G2.summary`) reports per-battery
health, cell voltages, temperatures and lifetime energy counters that the
regular telemetry doesn't carry.
